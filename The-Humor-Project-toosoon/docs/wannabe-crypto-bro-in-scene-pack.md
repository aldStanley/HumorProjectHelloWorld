## The central mapping

The sad girl's toxic situationship splits into TWO objects for the wannabe:
the **bag** he keeps buying back into (the compulsion) and the **recruiter
who ghosts him** (the avoidant other — the castable human for the cross-side
pair). The voice comes from the SCALE GAP: institutional vocabulary,
student-budget stakes.

| sad girl | wannabe bro |
|---|---|
| the situationship / him | the dream job (Goldman, Citadel, a16z, Blackstone) · his bag + the recruiter who ghosted him |
| rereading his texts | refreshing the portfolio he swore he'd stop checking · rereading "we'll keep your resume on file" |
| going back after saying she's done | staying "one more bonus season", every season · swearing off leverage Monday, redownloading the app Friday |
| his 3am "you up?" | the 3am price alert |
| telling her friends everything | screenshotting every trade to the group chat |
| fake "i don't care" energy | fake "crushing it" energy · fake "generational wealth" / "in it for the tech" energy |
| trying not to crash out | trying not to get liquidated |
| Hinge | Robinhood · Coinbase + the leverage app |
| his new girl | the kid from econ who actually got the offer |

The dream job is NAMEABLE: Goldman, Citadel, a16z, Blackstone are canon
names that may stand alone in captions (like the sad girl's bare "him") —
the object of the parasocial relationship is a firm, not a person.

## Open decisions flagged for correction

1. **Two toxic objects** — the bag (compulsion) + the recruiter (avoidant
   other). The recruiter is the cross-side human; alternative: go pure
   crypto-degen and drop recruiting entirely (loses the ghosting/rejection
   text analogs, which port the sad-girl material best).
2. **Recruiter kept ungendered** ("the recruiter", "they") — unlike the
   sad girl's canon "him".
3. **Coinbase + "the leverage app"** as the apps — could be Robinhood, a
   phantom wallet, or a sports book.
4. **The finance club** as a peer entity — gives campus texture (pledges,
   e-board, coffee chats); drop if too collegiate.
5. **Stakes addendum** (student-sized stakes) is a NEW line added to S6's
   MATCH THE STAKES — the one place this port adds a rule rather than
   swapping content.
